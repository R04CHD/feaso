3. Deposit Strategy & Section 27 Legal Risk Analysis

Evaluating Section 27 (*Sale of Land Act 1962* VIC) early deposit release feasibility against an APRA-regulated Deposit Bond.

HIGH RISK

⚠️ Section 27 Early Deposit Release

Requesting early release of buyer deposit on sale of 37 Kilmarnock Way.

  • 1. 28-Day Statutory Objection Window: Under Sec 27(1)-(3), the purchaser has 28 clear days to object after receiving the mortgage disclosure statement.
  • 2. Purchaser Objection Right: Purchaser conveyancer can object under Sec 27(2)(a) if net proceeds do not comfortably satisfy incoming mortgagees.
  • 3. Cross-Collateralization Issue: Under the combined $658k debt scenario, the high LVR virtually guarantees a purchaser objection.
⛔ Solicitor Directive: Do NOT rely on Section 27 to fund purchase deposit on 4 Terra Firma Circuit.
RECOMMENDED

🛡️ APRA-Regulated Deposit Bond

An irrevocable financial guarantee issued by an APRA insurer in lieu of cash deposit.

  • 1. Zero Immediate Cash Required: Satisfies General Condition 14 deposit obligations without unlocking home equity early.
  • 2. Speed of Issuance: Can be underwritten and issued within 24 to 48 hours for a small standard fee (~1-2% of deposit sum).
  • 3. Settlement Satisfaction: Full 10% purchase price is paid directly through PEXA electronic funds flow at final simultaneous settlement.
✓ Conveyancing Directive: Incorporate Special Condition 1 (Deposit Bond) into purchase contract.

4. Contractual Special Conditions Blueprint

Select a tab below to inspect the protective special conditions to be inserted into the Contract of Sale for **4 Terra Firma Circuit, Clyde**.

// SPECIAL CONDITION 1: DEPOSIT BOND EXECUTION
1. The Purchaser may satisfy the requirement to pay the deposit under General Condition 14 (or any part thereof) by delivering to the Vendor’s legal practitioner or conveyancer a Deposit Bond issued by an APRA-regulated financial institution or insurer in a form acceptable to the Vendor.

2. Delivery of the Deposit Bond within seven (7) business days of the Day of Sale shall constitute full satisfaction of the Purchaser's deposit obligation.

3. The Vendor shall only be entitled to draw upon or make a claim against the Deposit Bond in the event of a default by the Purchaser that results in the lawful rescission or termination of this Contract pursuant to General Condition 35.

4. At settlement, the Purchaser shall pay the full purchase price (inclusive of the deposit amount guaranteed by the Deposit Bond) to the Vendor via the Electronic Lodgement Network Operator (PEXA) in exchange for clear title and vacant possession.
Legal Purpose: Satisfies deposit requirements without needing upfront cash or bridging loans while protecting the vendor via an APRA-backed surety guarantee.
// SPECIAL CONDITION 2: SUBJECT TO UNCONDITIONAL SALE AND SIMULTANEOUS SETTLEMENT
1. This Contract is conditional upon the Purchaser entering into a binding, unconditional contract of sale for their existing property located at 37 Kilmarnock Way, Clyde North VIC 3978 on or before [Insert Date, e.g., 60 days from Day of Sale] ("the Sale Condition").

2. For the purposes of this Special Condition, a contract of sale shall be deemed "unconditional" only when all conditions precedent (including finance approval, building/pest inspections, and statutory cooling-off periods) have been satisfied or waived in writing.

3. If the Sale Condition is not satisfied by the date specified, either party may terminate this Contract by giving written notice to the other, whereupon the Deposit Bond shall be returned to the Purchaser, and neither party shall have any claim against the other.

4. Settlement of this Contract is contingent upon, and shall occur simultaneously with, the electronic settlement of the sale of 37 Kilmarnock Way, Clyde North VIC 3978. If settlement of 37 Kilmarnock Way is delayed through no fault of the Purchaser, the Settlement Date under this Contract shall be extended day-for-day for up to fourteen (14) business days without penalty.
Legal Purpose: Ensures you cannot be forced to settle on the purchase of 4 Terra Firma Circuit unless your sale on 37 Kilmarnock Way successfully clears all conditions and settles simultaneously.
// SPECIAL CONDITION 3: VENDOR BUMP CLAUSE AND PURCHASER RIGHT-OF-REPLY
1. Notwithstanding Special Condition 2, the Vendor may continue to market the Property for sale.

2. If the Vendor receives a bona fide competing offer from a third party that the Vendor intends to accept, the Vendor must give written notice to the Purchaser ("Notice to Waive").

3. The Purchaser shall have three (3) clear Business Days from receipt of the Notice to Waive to provide written notice confirming that:
    (a) The contract for the sale of 37 Kilmarnock Way, Clyde North VIC 3978 has become unconditional; or
    (b) The Purchaser waives Special Condition 2 and will proceed with the purchase unconditionally.

4. Time shall be calculated strictly using Business Days in Victoria (excluding weekends and public holidays). If the Purchaser provides notice under sub-clause 3 within the 3 Business Days, this Contract shall remain in full force and effect. If the Purchaser fails to provide notice, this Contract shall automatically terminate, the Deposit Bond shall be returned, and neither party shall have any further claim.
Legal Purpose: Protects against immediate contract cancellation if the seller receives another offer. Gives you 3 full Victorian business days to confirm your sale status before the seller can cancel.
// SPECIAL CONDITION 4: LINKED PEXA SETTLEMENT AND DISCHARGE PENALTY WAIVER
1. Both parties agree that settlement shall take place electronically via an Electronic Lodgement Network Operator (PEXA) and shall be linked with the electronic settlement of the Purchaser’s sale of 37 Kilmarnock Way, Clyde North VIC 3978.

2. If financial settlement cannot be completed on the due date due to administrative delays, workspace locking issues, or mortgage discharge processing constraints caused by incoming or outgoing mortgagees (and not by willful default of the Purchaser), provided all required documents were executed and PEXA financial tables populated by 2:00 PM on the Settlement Date:
    (a) Neither party shall be deemed in default under General Condition 32 or 35; and
    (b) General Condition 33 (Penalty Interest) shall be waived for a grace period of up to three (3) clear Business Days.
Legal Purpose: Eliminates default penalty interest if minor banking or PEXA processing delays occur during the electronic discharge of your split mortgage.
